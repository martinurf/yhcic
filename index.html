Who we are

A student-led investment community at Young Harris College. We study markets, write research, argue about valuation, and hold each other to a standard that does not depend on anyone's grade.

01

Discipline

A shared process for research and risk — sourced assumptions, defensible valuation, and a written record every thesis has to survive.

02

Perspective

Coverage that spans sectors and cycles, built by students who show up for the read, not just the headline.

03

Progress

Analytical habits that outlast a semester — modeling, judgment, and the discipline to revisit a position when the facts change.

Built for students who want to think beyond the ticker.

No prior finance experience required · All majors, all years
